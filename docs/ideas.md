# Oracle ideas

Current in 1.4: automatic champion/mode guides, configurable shortcut, tray controls, optional Windows startup and a compact manual guide picker. The owner removed Notebook and Champion Library; keep the product focused and do not reintroduce them without a request. The ideas below are historical proposals, not approved scope.

Ideas for the next versions, pending your choices:

1. **Patch impact for your pool.** A small brief showing which of your favorite champions and their core items changed, with links to the official notes. Prioritize your pool rather than a full patch wall.
2. **Post-game reflection.** Present your own match results, then ask one short question (“What cost the first death?”). Build a searchable journal with one improvement to carry into the next game.
3. **Mayhem combinations notebook.** Save augment combinations you have tried, attach personal observations and revisit them when you roll that champion again. Keep personal observations separate from provider win rates.
4. **Between-game reset.** Optional session time and break reminders after matches, with your chosen focus for the next queue. Never interrupt an active fight.
5. **Build comparison desk.** Compare two provider builds with sample size, patch and mode clearly visible. This needs reliable provider access; do not present scraped percentages without context.

Public match history features require a separate Riot API integration and policy review before shipping. No opponent tracking, hidden information, automated gameplay or speculative cooldown claims are part of this first version.
